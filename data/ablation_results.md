## D. Local-search attribution: QPSO + LS against PSO + LS

Both "+ LS" arms call the same memetic step (`app/core/local_search.py`: refine the swarm's best every 15 iterations with 2 passes of 2-opt + Or-opt, accept only a strict improvement, reinject into the worst particle, one 3-pass polish at the end). Same instances, seeds, particle count and iteration budget as B and C; only the base swarm differs.
