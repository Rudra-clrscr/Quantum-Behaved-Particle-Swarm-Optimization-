## D. Local-search attribution: QPSO + LS against PSO + LS

Does QPSO benefit from local search more than standard PSO does, or does local search help any swarm about equally? Both "+ LS" arms call the same memetic step (`app/core/local_search.py`: refine the swarm's best every 15 iterations with 2 passes of 2-opt + Or-opt, accept only a strict improvement, reinject into the worst particle, one 3-pass polish at the end). Same instances, seeds, particle count and iteration budget as B and C; only the base swarm differs.

Fitness is mean ± std over **feasible runs only**, with the feasible count beside it; an arm with no feasible run has no mean. Δ is the difference of those means, negative when QPSO + LS is better.

| Customers | QPSO alone | QPSO + LS | PSO alone | PSO + LS | Δ (QPSO+LS − PSO+LS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 40 | 1325.2 ± 0.0 (1/5) | 1029.2 ± 16.6 (5/5) | 1396.7 ± 47.3 (5/5) | 1020.8 ± 8.0 (5/5) | +8.4 (+0.8%) |
